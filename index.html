01About

I'm interested in industries that take a decade to move, and the stories people tell about the ones who try to move them.

I work in aerospace manufacturing in Wichita, where the industry has been building airplanes for a century. It's where I learned how large industrial programs actually run — schedule, tooling, quality, and the distance between a design and a delivered part. Alongside that I run a private holding company through which I pursue real estate, federal contracting, and early-stage industrial concepts.

Most of what I do outside of work I taught myself — reading into technical fields until I could argue with people who do them professionally, running the numbers on deals, and building the thing rather than talking about it. That's taken me from carbon capture chemistry to acquisition financing to feature-length screenwriting, usually in the same week.

Energy
FILE B
Sustainable Aviation Fuel

I've spent considerable time on the Power-to-Liquid pathway — direct air capture CO₂ paired with green hydrogen and Fischer-Tropsch synthesis to produce drop-in jet fuel. My work has centered on the U.S. Gulf Coast, where captured CO₂, cheap renewable power, existing pipeline infrastructure, and airline demand overlap in a way they do almost nowhere else.

I'm interested in the project development side specifically: assembling the feedstock, technology, and offtake agreements that turn a plausible pathway into a financeable project. If you work in DAC, catalysis, electrolysis, or airline fuel procurement, I'd like to hear from you.
